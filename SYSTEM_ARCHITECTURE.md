# GLOBE B2B — SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE

## SOURCE. GROW. SCALE.

Globe B2B is a global B2B sourcing, procurement, supplier verification and business connection platform designed to connect buyers with reliable suppliers and procurement opportunities.

---

## 2. CORE USERS

### Buyers
Businesses, entrepreneurs, organizations and individuals requiring products, suppliers or procurement services.

### Suppliers
Manufacturers, wholesalers, distributors and verified businesses capable of supplying products or services.

### Globe B2B Team
Internal staff responsible for sourcing, verification, procurement, sales, logistics, customer support and administration.

### Strategic Partners
Logistics companies, financial institutions, manufacturers, distributors, consultants and other organizations supporting the Globe B2B ecosystem.

## 4. REQUEST LIFECYCLE

1. SUBMITTED
2. UNDER REVIEW
3. SOURCING
4. SUPPLIERS IDENTIFIED
5. VERIFICATION
6. QUOTATION
7. CLIENT REVIEW
8. NEGOTIATION
9. PURCHASE ORDER
10. PROCUREMENT
11. SHIPPING
12. DELIVERY
13. COMPLETED
14. CLOSED

---

## 5. TRUST & VERIFICATION PRINCIPLE

Globe B2B is built around trust.

The platform should maintain records showing:

- Who the buyer is
- Who the supplier is
- What was requested
- What was quoted
- What was agreed
- What was purchased
- What was shipped
- What was delivered

Important business actions should be traceable.

## 7. SECURITY PRINCIPLE

The system must use controlled access.

Different users should only access information appropriate to their role.

Core roles include:

- SUPER ADMIN
- ADMIN
- PROCUREMENT
- SALES
- VERIFICATION
- LOGISTICS
- FINANCE
- CUSTOMER SUPPORT
- BUYER
- SUPPLIER
- PARTNER

---

## 8. PLATFORM DEVELOPMENT PRINCIPLE

Globe B2B will be developed progressively.

PHASE 1
Business validation and operational processes.

PHASE 2
Digital forms, customer acquisition and workflow automation.

PHASE 3
Structured database and internal management system.

PHASE 4
Buyer and supplier platform.

PHASE 5
Advanced procurement, logistics, payment and marketplace capabilities.

---

## 9. TECHNOLOGY PRINCIPLE

The technical architecture should remain modular.

Each major system component should be capable of being developed, tested and improved independently without breaking the entire platform.

The system should prioritize:

- Security
- Scalability
- Reliability
- Maintainability
- Data integrity
- User experience
- Operational efficiency

---

## 10. CORE BUSINESS PRINCIPLE

Globe B2B is not simply a marketplace.

It is intended to become a structured B2B sourcing, procurement and business infrastructure.

The long-term objective is to make sourcing easier, procurement safer, supplier discovery more reliable and business connections more efficient.
