# GLOBE B2B

## SOURCE. GROW. SCALE.

### GLOBAL SOURCING • PROCUREMENT • BUSINESS CONNECTIONS

## HOW IT WORKS

### 1. REQUEST
Tell us what product, service, quantity, quality, or specification you need.

### 2. SOURCE
We identify suitable suppliers and sourcing opportunities.

### 3. VERIFY
Where applicable, suppliers are reviewed using our verification and due-diligence process.

### 4. COMPARE
Available quotations, specifications, quantities, and commercial terms are evaluated.

### 5. PROCURE
Once the buyer approves the supplier and terms, procurement coordination begins.

### 6. DELIVER
Logistics and delivery coordination can be supported according to the agreed arrangement.
